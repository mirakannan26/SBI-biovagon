## Challenges and Solutions

Every AI biotech company faces real challenges. Pick TWO from the list and explain how your company would handle them:

### Challenge

| Challenge | |
|---|---|
| Not enough training data | Create synthetic sample data using mathematical approaches. |
| AI prediction is wrong / biased | Further unsupervised learning will have to be done to train the AI model to predict accurately. This can be done by providing more test samples after training AI with a wide range of datasets. |
| Regulators (FDA) don't trust AI-only results | Validate the results generated by AI by experimental approaches or by solid proof. |
| Patients from different ancestries respond differently | Re-evaluate the designed drug and the receptors it binds to. If it works for the remaining individuals tested apart from the specific group, then the drug can be labelled against a specific group as a restrictive measure, with reference to FDA guidelines. |

### My two chosen challenges + how I'd solve them

For the issue of not having enough training data and for patients from different ancestries responding differently, synthetic models like Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and Diffusion Models on small datasets can be used to generate highly realistic, synthetic molecular profiles or medical images.

Perturbation simulation can be used to simulate mutations and check their effects rather than experimentally testing on samples of diverse groups in the population to then modify the drug according to the simulated mutation for it to work on a variety of individuals.
