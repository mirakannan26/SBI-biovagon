## Part A: Access RNAcentral

1.      Go to [https://rnacentral.org](https://rnacentral.org)

2.      Search for 'Human XIST' or 'Homo sapiens XIST'.

3.      Open the relevant RNA entry.

 Q3: What information can you learn about an RNA molecule from RNAcentral?
 
 
 
Answer: It is possible to find :

·        The number of species it belongs to

·        Number of publications on it

·        Gene location in chromosome

·        Orientation of the gene on the strand (reverse or forward based on polarity)

·        IGV visualisation of the gene in Ensembl

·        Taxonomic tree

·        Expression profiles in different tissues and organs of the body from studied experiments
