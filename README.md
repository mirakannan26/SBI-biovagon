# Bioinformatic research project
This branch contains the project template provided by Biovagon and the final project submitted as two separate files.
The final project has the research gap addressed after surveying a few research and review articles which was on the topic of EGFR gene mutations that cause Non small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) and the resistance developed to its treatment by a first-generation TKI drug, gefitinib. 
4 basic database tools were used for answering the research question and findings were reported after each, with necessary pictorial evidence.
The final result of this project was inconclusive due to lack of data and statistical insignificant results for the parameters checked for a specific Single nucleotide Polymorphism(SNP) variant.
The use of more tools and further research can boost findings in the connection between EGFR expression and gefitinib resistance caused by the specific SNP.
